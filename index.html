Read a research paper in 30 minutes

Most students read papers front to back and get lost in the methods. Read them in this order instead.

Download the free checklist

The reading order

  1. Read the title and abstract. Decide in two minutes whether the paper answers your question. If not, stop.
  2. Look at the figures and their captions. Most papers tell their main story here. Write one sentence per figure.
  3. Read the introduction's last paragraph. It states what the authors set out to test.
  4. Skim the methods for the design, not the details. Note the sample, the controls, and the main measurement.
  5. Read the results against your figure notes. Check that the text matches what you concluded from the figures.
  6. Read the discussion last, and read it critically. Look for claims the data does not support.

Quick test: can you state the paper's question, method, and main result in three sentences? If so, you have read it well enough.
